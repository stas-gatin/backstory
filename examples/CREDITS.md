# Example photos

Both photos are works of the U.S. federal government and in the public domain. The captions in
`examples.json` were written for this demo; they are staged examples of a false caption, not real posts.

| File | Photo | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `katrina.jpg` | Flooded New Orleans after Hurricane Katrina, 29 August 2005. U.S. Coast Guard, Petty Officer 2nd Class Kyle Niemi | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KatrinaNewOrleansFlooded.jpg) |
| `sendai.jpg` | A U.S. Navy helicopter over the coast near Sendai after the tsunami, 12 March 2011. U.S. Navy photo | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SH-60B_helicopter_flies_over_Sendai.jpg) |

The recorded answers in `replay/` hold what the agents read: titles, publication dates, short excerpts and
captions of the web pages where these photos appear, and the models' answers.
