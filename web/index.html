Real photo, wrong story?

Most viral fakes are real photos with a false caption: an old flood shown as today's, a fire from another country. Backstory's agents find where the exact photo or video appeared online before, date every copy, read how each page describes it, and check the caption against that record.

  • Every verdict cites its evidence, and code checks the proof
  • No record, no guess: the answer is "Cannot verify"
