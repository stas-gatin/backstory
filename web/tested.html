What the failures taught
  • H1: the judge was too eager. It took a copy published a few days before the claim, or one page describing the photo differently, as proof. Fix: a proof check in code. A "does not fit" must rest on a copy dated more than a week before the claim, or on two independent sites. False alarms were about half on new data (Test I, a different split).
  • H3: news posts rarely give a date. "First picture of a chemical attack in Kursk" names no date, so a copy from 2016 had nothing to beat. Fix: a post that presents media as news is taken as no older than 30 days before the post. On 14 posts no version had seen: 3 of 6 fakes caught, no false alarm.
  • H5 and H6: video stays hard. Two ideas, each tested A/B on fresh posts, gave no gain. For a third of fake videos, reverse search finds nothing for any frame. Backstory then says "cannot verify" and does not guess.
  • Every run is recorded. Each external answer is stored by a hash of its request. The examples in the repository replay without keys; the test runs' recordings hold third-party media and are kept locally.
